# NP Server Rules (verbatim)

NP server rules, verbatim from the public rules channel. Revisions: rules preamble 2022-08-15; in-game rules 2025-09-29; Discord rules 2023-07-26.


## Preamble (revised 2022-08-15)

Claiming you did not read the rules upon breaking them will not exempt you from repercussions. The server admin reserves the right to terminate or suspend accounts at the admins sole discretion without notice should said account upon gameplay, chat or any player activity whatsoever be determined as inappropriate and/or in violation of the spirit of the Game.


## In-game rules (revised 2025-09-29)

1. You may not cheat or purposely exploit server bugs (no cheat engine or obviously unintended non-vanilla Morrowind exploits, and because it has to be said; no self-PVP farming) nor may you knowingly accept items from others who have done so.
2. You may not conduct in racist or insensitive behavior towards others based on their race, sex, religion, etc. (You may not use non-dunmeri/real life slurs.)
3. You may not mass genocide NPC's/Creatures in populated towns in game. Also avoid killing individual NPC's/Creatures that are important to quests or player progression unless you specifically have a game-related quest to kill them.
4. You may not move actors of importance (such as travel or quest-related NPC's) or creatures from their origin location/cell unless the quest specifically requires doing so.
5. You may not display toxic behavior. (Including, but not limited to, chat and action toxicity, such as PVP griefing.) If you are frustrated by something, reach out to a staff member to discuss it.
6. You may not cause intentional harm to the server, such as lagging the server or frequently casting large area spells/frenzying various NPC's in one area.
7. You may not publicly display any form of ERP (Erotic Role Playing). This includes sexually suggestive/explicit/innuendo character names.
8. You may not put quest related NPC's to sleep (i.e., incapacitating them for extended periods of time by draining all of their fatigue).
9. You may not have more than 10 characters on the server, and may not simultaneously play more than 4 at the same time.
10. You may not discuss nor play on the server with a device that Morrowind was not originally released on (i.e., android devices).
11. You must report any duplicated items you have obtained, regardless of means, to a staff member as soon as possible.
12. Bypassing the chats profanity filter will result in a ban. Use the following chat command instead: /profanityfilter
13. You may not utilize lag (unintentional or intentional) as a means to exploit something on the server. (Such as lagging yourself into a cell you should not be in, or permanent summoning additional summoned creatures.)
14. You may not alter or discuss altering TES3MP's code or packets in an exploitative/advantageous manner. (This also includes altering OpenMW's code.)
