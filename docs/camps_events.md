## Mudcrab Camp (Emperor Crab)

Location: Small chain of islands southeast (isle south) of Seyda Neen  
Mobs: Mudcrab  
- Boss: **Emperor Crab** — drops: Shield of the Crab God (rarest), Treachery glove (left), Deceit glove (right), Ring of Raven Eye, Mudcrab Tome, 25 crab meat, gems, 15 tokens (every time), Auriel's Shield (observed 2020) — Tier 2 world boss. Observed respawning after 3-4 hours, longer than other bosses. Can reset if it wanders about 100 feet from its spawn point, respawning at the spawn spot with full HP; the server dev said the reset is probably related to its large size, similar to the Skree boss.
- Regular mobs drop crab meat, gems, tokens (rarely), and mudcrab familiar tomes (rarely).
- Level range 1-11 (Beginner); quest-giver Shel Collector Doril at Arrille's Tradehouse in Seyda Neen trades 3 Mudcrab Shells for 22 Gold and 1,960 EXP; approx. 206 EXP per mob.
- Original resistances observed (2020-10): 100% magicka resist (weakness-to-magicka stacking does not work), 100% or near-100% poison resist, and around 25% reflect; shock and melee damage are noticeably more effective than frost, while fire performs about the same as melee.
- Using 3 shock rings each doing 100 damage over 2 seconds plus a melee player using Umbra, killing the Emperor Crab took about 40 minutes and roughly 3500 ring casts.
- Flagged change over time: the boss's resistances appear to have changed - poison, weakness, and absorb health effects are now always resisted, whereas it was previously killable much faster (one player reported nuking it to death in about 2 hits before the change).
- The server dev speculated that levitation plus an aerial attack might help prevent the boss from resetting/escaping during the fight.
- Recommended kill setups: exquisite rings enchanted with target frost damage 100pts on 10ft for as many seconds as the enchant can hold; or 110 Enchant skill plus around 10 rings enchanted with shock damage. A nuke ring setup of this kind works on every boss except Lavastorm, where a dedicated weakness ring might work better (untested).
- Because the boss is tough to solo for lower-level players, announcing it in chat and partying up shares the token drop; experienced players can solo it but it is tedious. World-boss difficulty scaling mainly manifests as resistances, and only the Emperor Crab has significant resistances.

## Morroween (Halloween Event)

Schedule: October (Halloween season); the Jack boss spawns once per night in Balmora at the 9th hour of in-game night - roughly once every 48 minutes of real time; later measured as spawning at 9pm in-game and despawning at 6am, respawning roughly every 50 minutes (48 minutes theoretical on vanilla timescale, ~51 minutes observed)

- Activating all the graves/caskets around Balmora grants a special title (Spooktober). Casket locations include: one in front of the Fighters Guild, one near the Wise Woman's house, one near the Thieves Guild, one near Caius's house, and the rest near the Temple. Later described as 21 total Halloween caskets in Balmora (8 in the East part of town, 13 in the West), granting the titles Mortician and Undertaker.
- You need to be in a group for everyone in it to get Story Teller points from a Jack kill.
- The Now You Know Jack achievement (surviving Jack's attack on Balmora) was obtainable in 2022 when Jack attacked Balmora directly, NOT obtainable in 2023 when Jack moved to his own realm, and obtainable again in 2024 even though Jack still attacks from his own realm.
- If the Jack boss gets stuck while phased (ghost mode) because the totems have disappeared, having everyone leave the cell and re-enter seems to un-bug the totems.
- An efficient trick-or-treat travel route (Strider to Vivec/Molag Mar/Suran, jump to Pelagiad, jump to Seyda Neen, Strider to Gnisis/Khuul, boat around Dagon Fel/Tel Mora/Vos/Sadrith Mora/Ebonheart/Hla Oad/Gnaar Mok, jump to Ald Ruhn, mage-teleport to Caldera) costs under 500 gold total with high Personality.
- Jack has a normal shield effect and some reflect; damage-attribute rings landed with little reflected, suggesting his reflect is not very high. Avoid self-cast offensive enchants/spells that could reflect and kill you; if you fully resist the element you attack with, his reflect cannot hurt you back with it.
- Weakness-to-magicka/Absorb-health ring spam had no noticeable effect on Jack; weakness-effect stacking does not work on him, but a Weakness to Magicka + Absorb Health + Damage Health enchant combo still deals damage. He seems to resist magic effects fairly well.
- Hitting Jack seems to require higher Agility/weapon skill: a character with 45 Agility/81 skill was missing him, while 126 Agility/110 skill hit reliably.
- Jack did not appear to regenerate health during the fight, and he has a kill/despawn timer - he goes invisible for a while (undetectable even with Detect Creature) then disappears.
- Removing spam/AoE quickkey items before landing the killing blow helps avoid accidentally killing your whole party via reflect.
- Nordic Silver Spears with Magicka Weakness plus a weakness-spam build took Jack down in about 1 minute 30 seconds; landing even a few hits at the very start counts for participation loot.
- Due to desync, Jack's displayed health can lag behind actual health - players sometimes see him die at what looks like half HP remaining, and the desync can cause missed loot. The server dev confirmed Jack's health was too high (2021) because a health change was not accommodated alongside a related mechanic change, and said it would be nerfed slightly on the next reset.
- If a group attacks Jack continuously, he gets stagger-locked and cannot cast his attack spell. Casting Demoralize on Jack causes him to flee.
- Jack is not resistant to Fire, making it the best element to use against him.
- Decrepit Bones from Jack can be fed to Horky at the Balmora River for masks or Gastric Cloud; each reward's price starts at 5 bones and increases by 5 bones for every additional item of that type already obtained; bones cannot be spent after the event ends. The gastric cloud acts like the sparkler item but applies a poison effect instead.
- During the 2020 event, petting Horky through the interaction menu gives the option to buy racial masks and the fart wand, while hugging him unlocks a separate, larger purchasable inventory (masks, weapons, clothing, familiar tomes).
- A special-event item cannot be handed off to another player after the fact; the intended recipient's character had to be online during the original event to receive it (server dev, regarding a sparkler event item).
- If the event's rare unique item drop is not obtained by many players in a given year, it may return the next Halloween (speculation).
- Drops: 8-28 Decrepit Bones (always), Morroween Hide Shirt (semi-rare; 75 enchant capacity), Wings of the Queen of Bats (semi-rare; two-handed axe with drain-health-on-strike enchantment), Unenchanted Daedric Longspear (semi-rare; 11.8ft reach, 10-50 damage), Ring of Namira (rare; constant 15% reflect), Bonekeeper Chestguard (very rare; medium armor that summons a skeleton minion with a daedric weapon for 10 seconds when the wearer is hit, 15-second cooldown between summons)

# Camp & event facts

- **camp difficulty**: Rough camp difficulty ranking (1=easiest, 13=hardest) per a 2025-05 player list: 1 mudcrab, 2 redwolf, 3 kagouti (easy, doable early); 4 skeleton, 5 aleft (dwemer), 6 ogrims, 7 hunger, 8 twilight, 9 druid (similar difficulty, roughly level 40-50, mainly needing magic resistances); 10 frost giant, 11 dreugh, 12 blood matrons crypt, 13 Tomb of the dessicator (hit hard and/or have high resistances/reflect, not recommended below level 60 without gear prep).
- **camp difficulty**: Camps/bosses can also be tiered as: entry/novice level includes the Red Wolf camp; adept level includes Aleft (dangerous even to mid-level-geared characters); expert level includes camps dropping level 50/60 items such as Dreugh, Druids, and Tomb (of the Snow Prince).
- **camp design**: Grind camps exist so players can farm MXP, intended primarily as post-level-60 progression content.
- **camp level ranges**: A compiled level-range table (2024-08) lists: Mudcrabs 1-11 (Beginner), Red Wolves 12-20 (Low End), Kagoutis 21-29 (Low End), Skeletons 31-39 (Mid-High End), Ogrims 46+ (Mid End), Twilight Matriarchs (High End), Druids (Mid-High End), Hungers 46+ (Mid-High End), Automatons/Aleft 46+ (Mid-High End), Frost Giants 56+ (High-High End), Scions (High-High End), Horrors (High-High End), Dreughs (Mid-High End).
- **camp maps**: Multiple community-made maps track camp locations over time: one map went through versions v1-v5 between 2023-02-23 and 2024-03-02, progressively adding Gertrude, Mudcrab, Kagouti, Hunger, Skeleton, Ogrim, Winged Twilight, Dwemer Centurion, Ole' Fangtooth, Red Wolf, Frost Giant, Muck Dreugh, and Unkempt Druid camps. Updated compilations from 2024-09-24 and 2025-05-02 mark substantially the same full roster across Vvardenfell and the northern Solstheim/Sheogorad island, plus Blood Matron's Crypt and Tomb of the Desiccator.
- **world bosses**: A 2020 map pins the approximate locations of all six then-known server bosses (Icy Jack, Gertrude, Inferno, Matches, Shadowgale, Bishop O-5) across Vvardenfell.
- **world bosses**: Confirmed grind-camp bosses include Gertrude, Ol' Fangtooth, and Zin the Dessicato.
- **boss respawns**: Boss respawn timers server-wide range from 2 to 8 hours depending on the boss.
- **respawn timers**: The server dev confirmed the respawn timer for fixed/static item spawns (e.g. Daedric Greaves at Dren Plantation) is random rather than fixed.
- **camp bugs**: The server dev said camp bosses dropping nothing at all should not normally happen and indicates a glitched spawn. Rarely, a camp boss monster shrinks in size and visually blends in with the regular mob NPCs.
- **boss stats**: An early (2020) camp boss HP estimate was roughly ~1000 hp (speculation).
- **boss fight tactics**: It is easy to accidentally hit another player at the newer boss fights, especially the newer fire boss, when minion waves respawn and players scatter to kill them.
- **boss fight tactics**: At the newer bosses, having one player spam Calm on nearby mobs keeps them from attacking the group, letting parties of 4 or fewer manage adds safely; a mage can use fortify summons so their summoned creatures take the aggro of nearby mobs. Players also kill respawning minions partly to reduce lag caused by their AI.
- **camp loot**: Creature camps have added many new items that boost magicka (dev-stated).
- **camp loot**: A boss/camp drop can be used to craft glamour items that make armor pieces (e.g. greaves, helm) turn invisible when worn.
- **storyteller**: Storyteller-related bosses spawn at the grindcamps, but with rarer spawn rates than the regular grindcamp mobs.
- **storyteller**: Killing a Bulbor grants 25 Commendation Points from the Storyteller, redeemable for goods by talking to him; Commendation Point rewards include custom items (books and a crossbow among them), purchasable stat boosts, and transformations.
- **boss Frost Claw**: Reflect works against the camp boss Frost Claw (and the Blood Matron crypt boss), but it can take about a half hour to kill some things with it; reflect is considered an easy strategy against Frost Claw, and some players instead defeat him by nuking his intelligence.
- **Jack variants**: If Smol Jack spawns, you can lure the NPC Storn into drowning him in a river. These Jack-variant claims are tagged under general camp discussion; their relation to the Morroween event boss Jack is not fully clarified in the source data.
- **Jack variants**: When fighting Flat Jack, his visual model is purely cosmetic - his actual hitbox does not move from where he normally stands.
- **farming spots**: Massama Caverns, just southwest of Dissapla Mine, is a glass and silver mine with over a dozen glass deposits and several dozen more silver rocks - one of the better glass-farming spots.
- **farming spots**: Druscathi is a popular looting location where items such as Blood of the Quarra Master potions can be found; it gets looted by others frequently.
- **farming spots**: Vos has hundreds of wickwheat pickable within a few minutes, plus marshmallow near the water.
- **farming spots**: Raiding the basement/storage areas in Mournhold (castle/Temple) yields a healthy stock of Kwama Eggs.
- **farming spots**: Pine trees have much higher density than other tree types, making Pine the best choice for farming wood/resin.
- **Kwama boss**: The Kwama boss loot table includes: Golden Kwama Egg, Kwama Gloves (left/right), Kwama Boots (hoppy boots), Minstrel's Lute, various loot bags, Resistance Robes, Large/Small Kwama Eggs, and a Daedric Sanctuary Amulet.
- **fishing loot**: The community fishing guide book incorrectly lists the Murky Fungus Robe as dropping in the Sheogorad region; a week of fishing there produced none, while an hour of fishing in Bitter Coast produced one.
- **farming spots**: Tel Fyr amulets can be farmed by anyone with access to Dregas Volar.
- **farming rates**: Using a nuke ring while auto-running and spam-attacking, a player reported getting 100 tusks in 30 minutes.
- **special spawns**: A notable silt strider spawns at a fixed location near Seyda Neen, between Addamasartus and Andrano Ancestral Tomb; the spawn location is near the Abaesen-Pulu Egg Mine, east of Andrano Ancestral Tomb, distinct from the regular Silt Strider travel post at Seyda Neen.

- **world mechanics**: The secret islands reportedly do not reset; the speculated reason is that spamming things like summoned creatures there would be harmful to the server.
- **farming spots**: Smuggler cave locations were mapped across Vvardenfell, with marked sites near Dagon Fel, the Vos area, Gnisis, Gnaar Mok, the Balmora area, the Pelagiad/Suran area, and Vivec.
- **events general**: Server events that grant rare items typically happen on a moment's notice, without advance warning (dev-stated).
- **events general**: The server dev will not fix Mournhold or Bloodmoon quest bugs until TES3MP can sufficiently run those areas smoothly.
- **events general**: Server event books (special skill books given out during events) reportedly grant increased skill gains compared to normal skill books (secondhand, unverified).

The server dev confirmed the pants auto-adding to a player's inventory on kill (without looting) is not scripted/intended, and that its inventory desynced on at least one occasion (nothing lootable), suspected due to random generated loot; the fix is letting the NPC despawn and respawn, sometimes requiring a server reset.
